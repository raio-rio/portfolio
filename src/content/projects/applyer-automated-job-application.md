## Overview

Applyer monitors remote job boards around the clock, scores new listings against my resume and preferences, and drafts tailored applications for strong fits. A scheduled watcher polls every 15 minutes, dedupes against known jobs, and pushes top matches to Discord. One-click AI drafts are built from the full job description plus resume context, with live progress streamed to the dashboard for every run.
